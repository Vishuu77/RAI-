# AgriBot 6-DOF Mechanical Chassis & Mounting Specifications
### **Autodesk Fusion 360 Parametric CAD Suite &bull; Er. Vishnu Ramachandra**

Designed to withstand continuous outdoor agricultural shock, soil ingress, and heavy vertical drill reaction forces without frame deflection.

## 1. Frame Architecture
- **Material:** 25mm x 25mm x 2.0mm Aluminum 6061-T6 Extruded Square Tubing with laser-cut 3.0mm gusset reinforcement plates.
- **Drivetrain Assembly:** Independent 4-wheel drive with rubber all-terrain chevron tread wheels (Ø 130mm, 60mm width) coupled via 6mm D-shaft brass hubs.
- **Vibration Isolation:** 4x Neoprene isolation dampers (Durometer 60A) decoupling vertical drill carriage reaction loads from sensitive electronics and camera mount.

## 2. Vertical Drill Lead-Screw Guide Rails
- **Lead-Screw:** Ø 8mm stainless steel 4-start lead-screw with 8mm pitch and anti-backlash brass nut.
- **Guide Rails:** Dual Ø 12mm hardened chrome-plated linear shafts with LM12UU linear ball bearings.
- **Drill Chuck:** Keyless 1.5mm - 10mm chuck mounted directly to Johnson DC motor output shaft with hardened set-screw coupling.

## 3. Carousel Sapling Dispenser
- 6-chamber rotary carousel manufactured from 4.0mm laser-cut acrylic / 3D-printed PETG.
- Gravity-drop chute aligned directly concentric with drilled soil cavity upon full Z-axis drill retraction.
